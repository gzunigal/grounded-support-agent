# Nexo TCG — Frequently Asked Questions

## Opening hours

Nexo TCG is open Tuesday to Friday from 12:00 to 20:00, and Saturday and Sunday from 11:00 to
19:00. The store is closed on Mondays and on national public holidays. On tournament nights the
play area stays open until the event ends, but the counter closes at the regular time.

## Location

The store is located at Avenida Providencia 1234, local 12, Providencia, Santiago, Chile. The
nearest metro station is Manuel Montt (Line 1), a 5-minute walk away. There is no parking on site;
public parking is available one block away.

## Membership

The Nexo TCG membership costs CLP 10,000 per year and includes a 5% discount on sealed product,
priority access to pre-order slots, and one free entry to a weekly tournament each month. You can
join at the counter or online with your customer account.

## Store credit

Store credit is added to your customer account and can be used on any product in store or online.
Store credit does not expire. Store credit cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Card condition evaluation

Our staff evaluates the condition of loose cards at the counter, free of charge, using the
standard Near Mint, Lightly Played, Moderately Played and Heavily Played scale. This evaluation is
used to set our purchase price for singles and to price singles in store.
